Batched backend protocol

HostBatchBackend is the maintained ordinary-array implementation. AcceleratorBatchBackend is a fail-closed adapter contract: an extension supplies its launcher and operation set, and begins at DocumentedBoundary unless stronger evidence is recorded. External adapters are intentionally absent from the maintained registry.

broadcast_accelerator is the scoped maintained operation: Gaussian RWMH over broadcast-compatible arrays with explicit Gaussian and uniform events. Tested refers to host-array conformance with scalar Reference, not a claim about any particular GPU runtime.

Independent-chain execution owns one explicit seed per state. Device transfer, RNG realization, reductions, callback support, and floating-point behavior remain obligations of each accelerator adapter.
