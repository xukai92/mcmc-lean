Formalization architecture

This document shows how the formalization is layered from mathlib's measure-theoretic foundations through the implemented RWMH and multinomial-HMC algorithms to the main meeting result of Xu, Fjelde, Sutton, and Ge.

The maintained overview is now the Lean-generated formalization dependency graph. Its nodes and edges are definitions in Mcmc.Docs.Graph, so they are typechecked with the formal project and regenerated by make generate-docs.

The two algorithm branches are concrete: RWMH and multinomial HMC are defined as mathlib kernels and proved correct before they are used in the coupled mixture. The final theorem does not assume opaque single-chain algorithms.

Where the finite layer fits

The chain state and momentum spaces are continuous, but a multinomial-HMC transition selects from a finite leapfrog trajectory. Finite probability and transport are therefore an internal component of the general-state kernel, not a restriction of the final theorem to finite state spaces.

flowchart LR
  Continuous[Continuous position and momentum]
  FiniteTrajectory[Finite trajectory indices<br/>from 0 through L]
  IndexLaw[Finite multinomial distributions]
  FiniteCoupling[Finite maximal or transport coupling]
  Output[Continuous coupled output positions]

  Continuous --> FiniteTrajectory --> IndexLaw --> FiniteCoupling --> Output

The reusable finite coupling and transport definitions live under formal/Mcmc/Finite/. Their measurable kernel lifts connect finite index couplings back to the continuous phase-space kernels.

Logical boundary of the main theorem

The important implication structure is

regularity + local strong convexity
  -> cutoff-wise positive-window HMC contraction
  -> local relaxed accessibility

local relaxed accessibility
  + RWMH exact-meeting small set
  + global drift for the selected HMC/RWMH kernels
  -> geometric exact lag-one meeting tail

Local strong convexity does not imply the global drift premise. Geometric meeting also does not, by itself, prove marginal convergence from arbitrary initial states. Those obligations remain explicit at theorem endpoints.

Main module map

  • formal/Mcmc/Kernel/MetropolisHastings.lean defines general-state MH.
  • formal/Mcmc/Kernel/RandomWalkMetropolisHastings.lean specializes it to Gaussian RWMH.
  • formal/Mcmc/Kernel/CoupledMetropolisHastings.lean constructs coupled MH with proved marginals.
  • formal/Mcmc/Hamiltonian/Leapfrog.lean defines the deterministic integrator.
  • formal/Mcmc/Hamiltonian/MultinomialHMC.lean constructs the multinomial-HMC transition.
  • formal/Mcmc/Hamiltonian/Invariance.lean proves its invariance properties.
  • formal/Mcmc/Hamiltonian/CoupledMultinomialHMC.lean constructs coupled HMC with proved marginals.
  • formal/Mcmc/Hamiltonian/LocalContractivity.lean connects the repaired Xu conditions to local accessibility.
  • formal/Mcmc/Kernel/MeetingDrift.lean contains the abstract drift and meeting machinery.
  • formal/Mcmc/Hamiltonian/CoupledMixture.lean assembles the concrete mixture and Xu-style geometric meeting theorem.
  • formal/Mcmc/Kernel/UnbiasedEstimator.lean develops downstream estimator consequences.
